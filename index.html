The point of the game

Build sequences.
Take the lead.

Be the first player to own three completed sequences. A sequence is made of three or more consecutive cards of the same suit. The twist is Intercept: when an opponent reveals a sequence that connects directly to yours, you can take control of the combined run. One well-timed move can change the whole game.

What you need

Nothing complicated.

One deck

A standard 52-card playing-card deck. Leave the jokers out.

2–4 players

Play head-to-head or with a small group.

A little strategy

Think ahead, watch the table, and choose your moment.
